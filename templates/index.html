How It Works

A simple three-step donor identification process.

01

Register

Donor registers their personal information and verified blood group.

02

Scan Face

Use a phone or laptop camera to identify the registered donor.

03

Get Information

The system retrieves the donor's verified blood-group information.

About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is a web-based blood donor identification and management system. Face recognition is used only to identify registered donors. Blood-group information is retrieved from the database.
